## 1. What is being quoted

Four collection points, surveyed on site and reported on individually. Each
survey covers the catchment surface, the downpipes, the first-flush diverter
and the storage tank, and ends in one of three verdicts: serviceable, repairable,
or beyond economic repair.

The work is offered as a single indivisible piece. The four points share a
manifold, and a report on three of them would not support a decision about any.

### What it does not cover

- Repair work of any kind. This is a survey; the repairs it recommends would be
  quoted separately, once their extent is known.
- Water quality sampling, which requires an accredited laboratory.
- Any statutory filing with the Water Board's own regulator.

## 5. Timetable

Work begins within ten working days of acceptance and concludes within four
weeks of beginning, weather permitting — the rooftop survey cannot be carried
out in high wind, and any day lost to weather extends the end date by one day.

1. **Week 1** — site survey of all four collection points.
2. **Week 2** — downpipe, diverter and tank inspection.
3. **Weeks 3–4** — analysis, drafting, and delivery of the report.

The report is delivered as a PDF, with photographs, in English. A version in
another language can be supplied on request at no extra charge.

Those three drawings are ordinary files sitting beside this document, written
`![what the picture shows](assets/plate.svg)` — PNG, JPEG, SVG and WebP all
work. The width after one, `{width=48%}`, is a share of the column, which is
how two of them come to stand side by side.

What goes in the square brackets is read two ways at once, and both of them
matter. A picture standing alone in its paragraph becomes a figure — centred,
with those words set beneath it as the caption. Two written side by side, as
the pair above are, stay in the line they were written in and print nothing.
Either way the words are carried into the PDF as the picture's description,
which is what a screen reader announces in place of it, and what `pdf.standard`
requires under PDF/UA-1 and the `a` levels of PDF/A. So they are worth writing
even where they will not be seen: say what the picture says, not that there is
a picture.
